Envisioning Our Information Future

September 6, 2017

In 2015 I was invited to participate in a small #InfoFutures forum funded by the Institute of Museum and Library Services and hosted by the Simmons School of Library and Information Science.  We were tasked with envisioning the future of information and how to educate future information professionals for this emerging world.

The Chairs of the forum just published this white paper, Envisioning Our Information Future and How to Educate for It, partially based on our forum discussions. The paper also references the Beyond the Stacks podcast I was interviewed for around that same time.
